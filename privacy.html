Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scholin collects, uses and shares information when you use the Scholin app and website. By using Scholin, you agree to this policy.

Download PDFTerms and ConditionsBack to website

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Scholin, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Photos, such as profile photos, student photos and the photo taken when clocking in or out.
  • School records entered by schools, such as classes, attendance, scores, report cards and fees.
  • Payment records for fees and subscriptions. Payments are processed by Paystack and we do not store your card details.
  • Vacancy details posted by users, such as job details, location and contact details.
  • Device and usage information, such as device type, app version and error logs.

How we use your information

  • To provide and maintain Scholin.
  • To manage your account and what you can access.
  • To process payments.
  • To send notifications about announcements, fees and attendance.
  • To keep Scholin secure and to improve it.
  • To contact you and give you support.

Who can see your information

Each school's information is kept separate and is only seen by people in that school, based on their role. Parents see only their own children.

Vacancies posted on the vacancy page are public. This includes the job details and the name and contact details chosen by the person who posted.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information when the law requires it or when a public authority makes a valid request.

Service providers

We use trusted companies to run Scholin, such as Supabase for the database and login, Cloudinary for images and Paystack for payments. They only handle information so they can provide their service to us.

Children's information

Schools enter information about their students and are responsible for having permission to do so. If you are a parent and have a question about your child's information, please contact your child's school or contact us.

Security

We work to protect your information, but no method of storage or transfer over the internet is completely secure. Please keep your login details private.

Keeping your information

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide Scholin or as the law requires. You can ask us to correct or delete your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, use the support option inside the Scholin app or the official Scholin channels.
